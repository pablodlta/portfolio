AeroStream: Engineering Case Study

Lead CAD & Mechanical Design Engineer | Academic Project[span_93](start_span)[span_93](end_span)[span_94](start_span)[span_94](end_span)

1. Mechanical Architecture & Subsystems

The project addresses domestic blending inefficiency by consolidating blending and drinking into a single modular vessel[span_95](start_span)[span_95](end_span). The drive base houses a brushless DC (BLDC) motor inside a 3 mm thick recycled 6000-series aluminum chassis with an integrated 2 mm torsional rib matrix[span_96](start_span)[span_96](end_span). Torque transfer is executed via a precision-machined PEEK polymer coupling threaded with an internal M8 concentric tap, ensuring zero rotational eccentricity to extend bearing lifespan[span_97](start_span)[span_97](end_span).

2. Anthropometric Jar & Mechanism Design

Geometric sizing was validated against European anthropometric hand percentiles[span_98](start_span)[span_98](end_span):

  • Jar body: 245 mm height, 100 mm max outer diameter, narrowed to a 75 mm waist for direct single-hand consumption[span_99](start_span)[span_99](end_span).
  • Handle: 110 mm vertical clearance accommodating P95 male metacarpal width (95 mm), 40 mm knuckle gap, and a 32 mm oval cross-section for torque application[span_100](start_span)[span_100](end_span).
  • Docking interface: 15 mm high threaded zone with a twist-and-lock safety interlock, restricting user rotation to 1.5 turns[span_101](start_span)[span_101](end_span).
  • Dual lids: One 35 mm manual vacuum lid with a 45 mm palmar button (5 mm travel stroke) and an ergonomic sport lid with a 28x24 mm elliptical drinking spout offset by 30 mm[span_102](start_span)[span_102](end_span).

4. Lifecycle Assessment (LCA) & DFD Optimization

Using SolidWorks Sustainability, a piece-by-piece baseline LCA was mapped on the SMEG BLF01 (500 kg CO2e, 6500 MJ energy, 93% landfill rate)[span_109](start_span)[span_109](end_span). AeroStream directly mitigates these hotspots by replacing virgin cast aluminum with >75% recycled aluminum, eliminating transoceanic resin shipment loops, and implementing Design for Disassembly (DFD)[span_110](start_span)[span_110](end_span). Fastening relies entirely on 4 metric M4 stainless screws accessible beneath friction-fit cork feet, enabling tool-free servicing and magnetic metal extraction during recycling[span_111](start_span)[span_111](end_span).

5. 100% Plastic-Free Packaging Logistics

The packaging system eradicates expanded polystyrene (EPS) and single-use PE bags[span_112](start_span)[span_112](end_span). The unit is housed in a 500x350x180 mm FSC-certified Kraft folding box lined with a custom 3 mm molded cellulose pulp tray, achieving full Class A recyclability under European PPWR packaging regulations[span_113](start_span)[span_113](end_span).

Koky: Engineering Case Study

Lead CAD & Packaging Engineer | Academic Project[span_114](start_span)[span_114](end_span)[span_115](start_span)[span_115](end_span)

1. Functional & Technical Constraints

The goal was to design an impact-resistant, ergonomic cosmetic container for pet care professionals and home users[span_116](start_span)[span_116](end_span). Glass was ruled out to mitigate shattering risks around animals[span_117](start_span)[span_117](end_span). Aluminium was selected for the primary container due to its chemical inertness with fragrance formulations, infinite recyclability, and low wall thickness capabilities[span_118](start_span)[span_118](end_span).

2. CAD Surface Modeling & Parametric Tree

The geometric architecture was developed through precise procedural modeling[span_119](start_span)[span_119](end_span):

  • Base body transitioned from a cylindrical footprint to a faceted gem cross-section using multi-plane guide curves (Loft/Recubrir)[span_120](start_span)[span_120](end_span).
  • Tactile grip area created by two opposed helical trajectories (clockwise and counter-clockwise sweeps) arrayed via circular body patterns and bounded by an extruded cut frame[span_121](start_span)[span_121](end_span).
  • Cap designed via guide curves matching the bottle's hexagonal facets; animal paw identifiers embossed on dual faces using tangent sketch projections (Wrap/Envolver)[span_122](start_span)[span_122](end_span).
  • Threadless coupling achieved through calibrated friction grooves modeled via swept cuts across the mating parts[span_123](start_span)[span_123](end_span).

UCAchew: Engineering Case Study

Product Developer & Material Selection Specialist | Academic Project[span_134](start_span)[span_134](end_span)[span_135](start_span)[span_135](end_span)

1. Ethological & Biomechanical Constraints

Canine mastication fulfills critical neuro-behavioral functions, promoting dopamine release in the striatum and reducing cortisol levels[span_136](start_span)[span_136](end_span)[span_137](start_span)[span_137](end_span). Mechanically, the product was sized for medium-sized breeds exerting canine bite forces between 235 and 328 PSI (local peak stresses reaching ~17 MPa at dental contact points)[span_138](start_span)[span_138](end_span)[span_139](start_span)[span_139](end_span). The 200 mm symmetric bone profile integrates R20 flared lobes for front paw stabilization and an ergonomic waist for lateral molar shearing[span_140](start_span)[span_140](end_span)[span_141](start_span)[span_141](end_span)[span_142](start_span)[span_142](end_span).

2. Ashby Material Screening Methodology

Candidates were mapped across tensile strength vs. elongation, Shore A hardness vs. tear strength, and density vs. cost charts[span_143](start_span)[span_143](end_span):

  • Eliminated Natural Rubber: High tear resistance but degraded by UV exposure and environmental ozone[span_144](start_span)[span_144](end_span).
  • Eliminated Polyamide (Nylon): Excessive hardness (>95 Shore A) risking tooth micro-fractures, brittle behavior, and microplastic shed[span_145](start_span)[span_145](end_span).
  • Eliminated Silicones & Soft TPEs: Shore A <50 resulted in high puncture susceptibility and accelerated fragmentation[span_146](start_span)[span_146](end_span).
  • Selected Material: Aliphatic Ether-based TPU (Shore A80), providing high tear strength (25–35 kN/m) and 300–550% elongation to prevent shear tears[span_147](start_span)[span_147](end_span)[span_148](start_span)[span_148](end_span).

1. Functional & Anthropometric Problem Framing

Targeted at compact urban households reconciling remote work with childcare[span_168](start_span)[span_168](end_span). The unit provides physical and visual zoning without permanent structural alterations[span_169](start_span)[span_169](end_span). Anthropometrically configured to support dual ergonomic postures: seated work (70–76 cm desk surface with knee clearances) and standing work (95–115 cm elbow height), stabilized by 550 mm solid timber outriggers with an anti-tip base radius (R300)[span_170](start_span)[span_170](end_span).

2. Kinematic Hinge Architecture & Rails

The articulation mechanism solves spatial flexibility through an integrated structural profile[span_171](start_span)[span_171](end_span):

  • Cylindrical stainless steel core pins acting as continuous axial shafts, expanding folding travel from 240° to a full 360° perimeter rotation[span_173](start_span)[span_173](end_span).
  • Accordion collapse geometry enabling total fold-down without removing panel segments[span_174](start_span)[span_174](end_span).

4. Flat-Pack Logistics & EPAL 1 Palletization

The kit is engineered to pack strictly into a 660x920x574 mm corrugated carton (600 kg BCT rating) arranged in 5 horizontal layers: Layer 1 stores 9 stacked plywood panels (124 mm); Layer 2 nests 2 packs of aluminum hinge-rails (110 mm); Layer 3 contains 3 timber support feet (180 mm); Layer 4 holds the stainless steel hinge pins inside rigid protective tubes (90 mm); and Layer 5 provides a 50 mm impact-absorbing top cushion[span_183](start_span)[span_183](end_span). Packaged to maximize volume utilization on standard EUR/EPAL 1 pallets (1200x800 mm) with a 3-tier stacking threshold during freight transit[span_184](start_span)[span_184](end_span).

5. User Testing & Design Iteration

A qualitative focus group session evaluated early prototypes against domestic usability criteria[span_185](start_span)[span_185](end_span). Key insights drove the widening of the hinge barrel diameter by 10 cm to eliminate bind-up during 360° rotation, the standardization of a single keyhole grid across all modular panels, and the addition of rubber-treaded retractable casters on the base outriggers[span_186](start_span)[span_186](end_span).
