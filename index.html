AeroStream: Engineering Case Study

Lead CAD & Mechanical Design Engineer | Academic Project

1. Mechanical Architecture & Subsystems

The project addresses domestic blending inefficiency by consolidating blending and drinking into a single modular vessel. The drive base houses a compact motor, gearbox, and sealing arrangement designed for serviceability and reduced material use.

2. Anthropometric Jar & Mechanism Design

Geometric sizing was validated against European anthropometric hand percentiles:

  • Jar body: 245 mm height, 100 mm outer diameter, narrowed to 75 mm waist for direct single-hand consumption.
  • Handle: 110 mm vertical clearance for P95 male metacarpal fit, with a 32 mm oval cross-section for torque application.
  • Docking interface: 15 mm threaded zone with twist-and-lock safety interlock, restricting user rotation to 1.5 turns.
  • Dual lids: manual vacuum lid and ergonomic sport lid designed for safe use and effective drinking flow.
3. DFM, Materials & Surface Treatments
Rotary Blade Subsystem Material: AISI 316 Stainless (>75% Rec.)
Process: Cold Stamped & CNC Sharpened
Finish: High-Vacuum PVD Diamond-Like Carbon (DLC)
Chassis & Gaskets Chassis: High-Pressure Die Cast Al 6000 + Anodized
Gaskets: Food-Grade Agglomerated Natural Cork
Jar: Injection Stretch Blow Molded Tritan Renew
4. Lifecycle Assessment (LCA) & DFD Optimization

Using SolidWorks Sustainability, a piece-by-piece baseline LCA was mapped against a conventional blender. The concept proposed clearly lower material intensity, simplified disassembly, and improved end-of-life recovery.

5. 100% Plastic-Free Packaging Logistics

The packaging system eliminates expanded polystyrene and single-use PE bags. The unit is housed in a FSC-certified Kraft folding box designed for protective transport and reduced volumetric waste.

Koky: Engineering Case Study

Lead CAD & Packaging Engineer | Academic Project

1. Functional & Technical Constraints

The goal was to design an impact-resistant, ergonomic cosmetic container for pet care professionals and home users. Glass was ruled out to mitigate breakage risk, while maintaining premium perceived quality.

2. CAD Surface Modeling & Parametric Tree

The geometric architecture was developed through precise procedural modeling:

  • Base body transitioned from a cylindrical footprint to a faceted gem cross-section using multi-plane guide curves.
  • Tactile grip area created by opposed helical trajectories arrayed around the body and bounded by an extruded cut frame.
  • Cap designed via guide curves matching the bottle's hexagonal facets; paw icons embossed on two faces.
  • Threadless coupling achieved through calibrated friction grooves modeled across the mating parts.
3. DFM, Materials & Disassembly

Surface: Anodized / Cold Stamped
Dispenser Subsystem Polypropylene Core (Injection Molded)
Aluminium Outer Shroud (CNC Turning)
Toolless Separation for Circularity
4. Secondary Packaging & Palletization (ISO 3394)

The secondary packaging features a rigid hexagonal folding box with an interior molded pulp cushioning tray. This keeps the product resistant while fitting a realistic pallet unit and handling flow.

5. Physical Prototyping

Full-scale functional prototypes were printed using FDM additive manufacturing in PLA. They were post-processed with metallic primers and selective enamel paint to validate form, feel, and assembly logic.

UCAchew: Engineering Case Study

Product Developer & Material Selection Specialist | Academic Project

1. Ethological & Biomechanical Constraints

Canine mastication promotes critical neuro-behavioral functions, including play engagement, stress reduction, and enrichment. The toy therefore needed to balance durability, safe chew behavior, and sensory feedback.

2. Ashby Material Screening Methodology

Candidates were mapped across tensile strength vs elongation, Shore A hardness vs tear strength, and density vs cost charts:

  • Natural Rubber was eliminated because of UV and ozone degradation.
  • Polyamide (Nylon) was eliminated due to excessive hardness, brittle behavior, and microplastic concerns.
  • Silicones and soft TPEs were excluded because low hardness caused puncture susceptibility and accelerated fragmentation.
  • Selected material: aliphatic ether-based TPU (Shore A80), balancing strength, elasticity, and safety.
3. DFM: Hollow Geometry & Blow Molding

Processing Temp: 170 °C – 220 °C
Wall Thickness: 5.0 mm (Uniform)
Raw Material Cost: 5 – 7 €/kg
Functional Attributes Weight Reduction for Throwing Ergonomics
Controlled Elastic Damping on Bite
Interior Hollow for Treat Dispensing
100% Regrind Circularity (Thermoplastic)
4. Safety Compliance & Canine Sensory Optimization

The formulation was designed to satisfy European EN 71-3 heavy-metal migration limits and FDA 21 CFR food-contact criteria, avoiding phthalates, BPA, PVC, chlorine, and latex allergens.

Modular Flat-Pack Divider: Engineering Case Study

Design Methodology & Product Engineering | Academic Project

1. Functional & Anthropometric Problem Framing

Targeted at compact urban households reconciling remote work with childcare, the unit provides physical and visual zoning without permanent structural intervention. The concept prioritizes flexibility, modularity, and easy storage.

2. Kinematic Hinge Architecture & Rails

The articulation mechanism solves spatial flexibility through an integrated structural profile:

  • Extruded aluminum guides combining panel slide channels with rotating hinge barrels.
  • Cylindrical stainless steel core pins acting as continuous axial shafts for 360° movement.
  • Accordion collapse geometry enabling total fold-down without removing panel segments.
3. DFM, Tooling & Modular Fastening Interface
Fastening Interface CNC Keyhole Slot Matrix
Toolless Drop-and-Lock Accessory Anchors
Reversible Panel Faces (Work / Play)
800x400 mm Cantilever Table + 63° Stiffener
Materials & Processes Panels: FSC Birch Plywood (CNC Milled)
Guides: Extruded Al 6000 + Color Anodized
Shafts: Ground AISI 304 Stainless Rods
Bases: Solid Hardwood CNC Turned/Routed
4. Flat-Pack Logistics & EPAL 1 Palletization

The kit is engineered to pack into a 660x920x574 mm corrugated carton arranged in five horizontal layers, balancing protection, stackability, and efficient delivery. This keeps the product practical for domestic installation and shipping.

5. User Testing & Design Iteration

A qualitative focus group evaluated early prototypes against domestic usability criteria. Key insights drove hinge widening and a more intuitive locking interaction to improve everyday use.
