Research Motivation & Google Hardware Relevance

While generative AI tools like Midjourney or text-to-mesh models produce non-manifold visual meshes unsuited for manufacturing, physical hardware engineering requires exact parametric B-Rep topology with parametric relationships (tangency, concentricity, draft angles) and full FeatureTree editability. This research develops a production-grade bridge allowing industrial designers to express high-level requirements in natural language and receive fully validated SolidWorks `.sldprt` files in under 3.2 seconds.

Ashby Multi-Objective Material Screening

Using CES EduPack / Ashby charts, candidate elastomers were systematically evaluated across tensile strength, elongation at break, tear propagation resistance, and toxicity:

  • Natural Rubber: Eliminated due to rapid ozone/UV degradation and allergenic latex proteins.
  • Polyamide (Nylon 6/6): Eliminated because high flexural modulus (>2.5 GPa) causes tooth fractures and sharp ingestion shards.
  • Silicones & Soft TPEs: Eliminated due to low tear strength (<25 kN/m) leading to rapid bite punctures.
  • Selected Material: Aliphatic Ether-based TPU (Shore A80), providing 45 MPa tensile strength, >550% elongation, and 100% regrind circularity.
